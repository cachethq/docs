## Overview

Cachet is an open source, self-hosted status page system.

Use Cachet to keep your customers and stakeholders informed during downtime and maintenance. Manage updates through the dashboard and customize your status page's interface.

<Warning>
    Host Cachet separately from your main application. Your status page needs to stay online when your application is down.

    Run Cachet on infrastructure independent of the application, web service, or SaaS product it reports on. Use a different server, provider, or region, with its own database and dependencies. If Cachet shares infrastructure with your product, the same outage can take both offline and prevent customers from reading status updates.
</Warning>
