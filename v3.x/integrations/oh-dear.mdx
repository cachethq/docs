Oh Dear monitors websites for downtime, broken links, and other issues. You can import its sites and incidents into
Cachet to display them on your status page.

To import your data, open **Oh Dear** in the integrations section of your Cachet dashboard.

Enter your Oh Dear status page URL, such as `https://status.ohdear.app`.
Cachet fetches the data from the `/json` endpoint.

<Tip>
    You can repeat the import. Cachet creates only components and incidents that do not already exist.
</Tip>

## Importing sites

To import your Oh Dear sites as [components](/v3.x/guide/components), enable **Import Sites**. Then select a
[component group](/v3.x/guide/components#component-groups) for all imported sites.

You can also choose to create a new component group for the imported sites. If you do not select a component group, the
sites are imported as ungrouped.

## Importing incidents

To import your Oh Dear incidents, enable **Import Incidents**. Cachet attempts to match Oh Dear's status codes to
Cachet's incident types.

<Note>
    The Oh Dear integration imports only the last 7 days of incidents.
</Note>
