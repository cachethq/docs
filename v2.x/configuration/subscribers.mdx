## Overview

Cachet can send notification emails to your status page's subscribers whenever an incident is created.

## Configuring subscribers

<Tip>
    Before enabling subscriptions, configure [mail](/v2.x/configuration/mail) and the [queue](/v2.x/configuration/queue).
</Tip>

Log in to your dashboard. In the **Application Setup** panel, enable **Allow people to sign up to email notifications?**

A **Subscribe** button appears in the footer of your status page.
